## Introduction

I had occasionally talked about doing a sunrise dive, but actually making it happen was not easy.

- We had to wake up long before sunrise to allow time for the drive and our pre-dive preparations.
- We would be entering the water almost entirely in the dark, so choosing a suitable entry point required extra care.

## The Shuanghe Crew

I did not expect my Northeast Coast dive buddies to be quite this committed. During my final week before leaving for Green Island, A-Ren, 7p_hhh, and Jiaqi all agreed to join me for a sunrise dive at Rose Garden.

After our last red-eye flight from Cologne, everyone once again had to recover quickly from the previous day's work. Fortunately, we all lived around Shuanghe, which made meeting up a little easier.

## Rose Garden

After diving at Rose Garden for the first time earlier that month, I felt that its entry point was relatively manageable and less risky in the dark.

When we arrived at the nearby parking lot, we discovered that the harbor was used by nighttime fishing boats. It was actually livelier at night than in the morning, which helped ease our nerves.

Once we reached the entry point, however, the shore break was no joke. Honestly, I wanted to call off the dive, but everyone went in anyway. With the help of our flashlights, we managed to kick out safely.

### Watching the Sunrise from the Sea

We expected the water to be pitch-dark, so we mostly held on to the float, chatted, and watched the colors of the sky slowly change.

For photos, we focused on split-level shots. Rose Garden is surrounded by land on three sides, which made the backdrop far more interesting than expected.

## A Second Dive at Longdong

Another reason we chose that day was that Yuting had arranged a training session with her instructor at Longdong. Somehow, even after completing two dives in one day, it was still only noon. 😂

We also invited Rosa to dive with us at Longdong. I should ask her to join us for freediving more often.

We brought along Akatsuki and Attack on Titan costumes to experiment with underwater. I still need to work out a better way to secure the fabric, since the floating material can easily cover the details that make the costumes recognizable.

I also showed 7p_hhh how to set up the float, although she may not have actually remembered it—and whether she will ever set one up on her own is another question.
